04 / Methodology

How the recommendation is calculated.

A weighted-sum method demonstrates how engineering priorities influence a preliminary material shortlist.

01 · Define criteria

Choose priorities for strength, weight, corrosion, temperature and cost.

02 · Property ratings

Each candidate has illustrative ratings from 1 to 5.

03 · Calculate score

Multiply each rating by its selected weight and normalize.

04 · Rank candidates

Sort materials from highest to lowest score.

Formula: Score = Σ (criterion importance × material rating) ÷ Σ (criterion importance). For low weight and low cost, a higher rating means more favorable.
